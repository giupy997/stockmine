    02

    Two streams of ETH, three destinations

    No reward token is minted in the background. The stocks are bought on Uniswap with ETH that actually came in, and a slice of the same ETH buys the project token back and burns it.

    Stream A

    Cut of every round

    7 % of the losing squares. Hard cap 15 % in the contract.

    Stream B

    Creator fees on Pons

    Phase one: the dev wallet collects them on Pons and sends them to the pot by hand. Phase two: the contract becomes the fee recipient and pulls them itself.

    Every epoch

    Pot → Uniswap → one stock

    First the burn slice is set aside. Then the keeper picks one allowed stock and a minimum amount out, and the rest of the pot is swapped in a single transaction.

      04

      What the contract allows

      We cannot

      • Touch the ETH players put on the grid. It is accounted apart from the pot.
      • Stop you from closing, drawing or claiming. Pause only blocks new deployments.
      • Raise the cut above 15 %, the burn slice above 30 %, or push the miners/stakers split outside 20–80 %.
      • Send the burn reserve anywhere except the token's own Pons curve and pool, the pair the Pons factory recorded, or back to the pot.
      • Mint anything. The token has a fixed supply from its Pons launch.

      We can

      • Choose which allowed stock an epoch buys, and the slippage limit.
      • Add or remove stocks from the allowed list (16 at most).
      • Tune cut, split, burn slice and unstake delay inside the hard bounds.
      • Decide when the buyback runs and with which slippage limit.
      • Redirect the Pons creator fees to another address. It is meant for a future version of the contract, but the code accepts any address.

      Know this

      • The draw uses the hash of a future L2 block. Players cannot predict it; the chain's sequencer in theory could.
      • In phase one the Pons creator fees pass through the dev wallet before they reach the pot. Every transfer is visible on-chain, but it is not enforced by code until the hand-over to the contract, which will be announced on this page.
      • The contract has not been audited.
      • This is a game of chance. You can lose all the ETH you put on the grid.
